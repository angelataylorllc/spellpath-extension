SpellPath

A Chrome extension that turns a topic you want to learn into a short, interactive choose-your-path story.

How it works

You pick a topic and a genre. SpellPath writes a story with checkpoints so you have to understand the material to keep going. Sign in with Google. The first three stories are free.

Plans

After the three free stories, subscribe monthly. Unused stories do not roll over. Checkout opens in Stripe.

$5 / month 20 stories $10 / month 50 stories

Use the same Google email in the extension as at checkout so your stories unlock.

Billing and canceling

Angela Taylor LLC sells SpellPath. Payments go through Stripe — we never see your card number.

To change or cancel a plan, sign in to the SpellPath extension and click Billing in the toolbar (next to sign out). That opens Stripe’s customer portal. You can also email hello@angelataylorllc.com.

Privacy

  • Google gives us your name and email so we know who is signed in.
  • Stripe stores payment details. We keep a customer and subscription id so your quota works.
  • Story text is generated by Anthropic (Claude Haiku) using our key. We keep the story so you can finish it.
